About

One job, done well

I design mechanical things for a living and write software for fun. The apps here started as itches I could not scratch with anything already on the Play Store, so I built them myself. They are made to do one job well, cost a few dollars once, and stay out of your data.

No ads, no subscriptions, no accounts. Nothing leaves your phone.
